Cheese balls

Description

A delicious and easy way to enjoy a cheese ball. You could substitute Colby, mozzarella, or Monterey Jack cheese for the Cheddar. Cut this recipe in half to make a smaller version. Serve with crackers and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (1 ounce) package ranch dressing mix
  • 2 ½ cups shredded Cheddar cheese
  • Chopped nuts

Steps

  1. Mash cream cheese in a medium sized bowl. Mix dressing mix and Cheddar cheese into cream cheese. Shape the mixture into a ball.
  2. Roll the cheese ball in the chopped nuts. Refrigerate covered until ready to serve.
