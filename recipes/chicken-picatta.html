Chicken Piccata Casserole

Description

I love chicken piccata, but I do not love cooking it on the stove. This oven-baked casserole recipe gives you the great piquant taste of chicken piccata without the splatters. It is more convenient and frees you up to tend to the other parts of dinner, like some tasty pasta, and maybe a vegetable or a salad.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup chicken broth
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 3 (8 ounce) skinless, boneless chicken breasts
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Coat a 9x13-inch casserole dish with cooking spray.
  2. Melt butter in a medium skillet over medium heat. Add flour and garlic; stir constantly until garlic is fragrant and flour and butter are well combined, about 1 minute. Stir in chicken broth and half-and-half. Bring to a boil, stirring frequently, until sauce is bubbly and thick, about 5 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat; stir in ½ cup Parmesan cheese, lemon juice, capers, salt, and black pepper; set aside.
  4. Slice each chicken breast lengthwise into 2 cutlets; place in a single layer in the prepared dish. Season with salt and black pepper. Spread sauce evenly over cutlets; top with lemon slices.
  5. Combine remaining ¼ cup Parmesan cheese and bread crumbs in a small bowl; sprinkle evenly over casserole.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the cutlets reads 165 degrees F (74 degrees C), 20 to 25 minutes. Garnish with parsley; serve warm.
